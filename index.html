Chapter II · The outfit

Find a crew worth riding with

In March 2024 I joined Fiserv, on the Developer Relations team for Clover, the payment devices you see on shop counters.

My job there is certification. Outside developers build apps for those devices, and nothing reaches a merchant until it has been tested and approved. I ride with each team through that process. I read their builds, test their payment flows and tell them plainly what has to change.

It taught me what I value most in a crew. Someone has to keep the camp running so everyone else can do the job they are good at. I like being that person.
